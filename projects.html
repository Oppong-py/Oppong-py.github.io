Building Age Classification from Satellite Imagery

GHANA DATA SCIENCE REGIONAL HACKATHON 2026 · KNUST · TEAM OF FOUR

Four days at KNUST with a team of four. The task: work out roughly when a building was put up, using nothing but 40 years of satellite readings of the ground it stands on. Train on Madrid, then make it work in Amsterdam with as few as five labelled examples per era.

The chart is the finding. We tested five sets of input features; on Madrid they rank one way and on Amsterdam in exactly the opposite order. Choosing a model the way every course teaches — best home score wins — picks the wrong one. We also caught the scoring we’d been handed inflating every result by 0.047, because a satellite pixel is smaller than a building and the standard split was putting half a roof in training and half in the test set.

We didn’t place. Two write-ups go through it properly: the leak we found in our own evaluation, and what transfer learning actually costs.

Task4-class, macro-F1, Madrid → Amsterdam
Data102,255 pixels · 40 years of Landsat
Madrid, honest CV0.5656 ± 0.0227
Amsterdam, 25 labels/class0.6220 ± 0.0105

03 · MOBILE2026

Arethos — Mental Wellness App

IN DEVELOPMENT · CO-FOUNDER · 3-PERSON TEAM

Building a cross-platform wellness app for KNUST students with two teammates: an AI-assisted journal, a mood tracker with sleep and energy logging, and a reflections tab that surfaces behavioural patterns in plain language.

Engineered the reflection engine on Groq for fast inference, and a custom haptic layer calibrated across nine iterations on Android hardware — expo-haptics only fires single-pulse vibrations there, so the fix was dropping to React Native’s native Vibration.vibrate(). The sleep slider uses a PanResponder with ref-pinned state to eliminate stale-closure bugs.

Fully offline-first: SQLite local storage, no user data ever leaves the device. Documented across 16 chapters covering every architectural decision.

Still in development — not released, and the repository stays private until it is. The write-up below is what the build has taught us so far, not a post-launch retrospective.

Solar-Powered Water Dispenser

TEAM OF 3 · OFF-GRID

Debounce logic prevents false triggers, with a buzzer for audio feedback and LED status indicators. The whole rig runs off-grid on solar, for deployment where mains power is unreliable.
