The short version

Three paragraphs  ·  the facts are in the panel on the right
Robotics got me started. Security kept me curious. Machine learning is where I’ve ended up.

I’m reading Computer Science at KNUST in Kumasi, second year of four, finishing in 2029. I speak English, and enough French to hold a conversation. I got here through robotics and cybersecurity. I spent secondary school tuning a line-following robot until it won nationals, and I ran the school’s cybersecurity club, teaching seven juniors. Both turned out to be the same job: watch what the system does, change one thing, watch again.

The constraints here shape what I build. The solar dispenser had to work where the power isn’t dependable. Arethos keeps everything on the device, so it works with no connection at all. Designing around that has become a habit.

Away from a keyboard I read, and work through maths problems because I find them fun. I also help younger students get started in robotics and security.
