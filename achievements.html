Sept 2026Machine learning

Ghana Data Science Regional Hackathon 2026 — Building Age Classification

Four days at KNUST with Kelly Nyarko, Hughes Tsikata and Antwi Yaa, organised by AstroXMat Research Laboratory with Hack4Dev. The task: work out roughly when a building was put up, using 40 years of satellite readings. Train the model on Madrid. Then make it work in Amsterdam. We didn’t place. Two things came out of it. We caught the scoring we’d been handed inflating every result by 0.047. And the feature set that scored best on Madrid turned out to be the worst one to carry across.
