Sept 2026Machine learning

Ghana Data Science Regional Hackathon 2026 — Building Age Classification

Four days at KNUST with a team of four, organised by AstroXMat Research Laboratory with Hack4Dev. The task: work out roughly when a building was put up using 40 years of satellite readings, train the model on Madrid, then make it work in Amsterdam. We didn’t place. The part worth keeping is that we caught the scoring we’d been handed inflating every result by 0.047, and found that the feature set scoring best on Madrid was the worst one to carry across.
